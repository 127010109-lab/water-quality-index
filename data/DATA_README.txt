DATA NOTE
=========
benchmark_water_samples.csv (500 rows) and monitoring_stations_50.csv (50 rows) contain SIMULATED values
generated within plausible ranges for five source types (bottled RO water, municipal tap, surface river,
deep borewell, industrial basin). Station coordinates are placed around the Thanjavur region for map demonstration.

They are used only to test and demonstrate the software. They are NOT field measurements, were NOT used to
train or calibrate any model, and do not validate AquaSafe against real water supplies.
Replace them with measured laboratory data for any real assessment.
